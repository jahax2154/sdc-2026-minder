# Domain definitions and non-negotiable boundaries

**DEF-01 — Personal preparation fit.** The degree to which a role aligns with a
user's demonstrated or explicitly reported capabilities, interests, relevant
experience, work preferences, and ability to undertake an initial preparation
milestone within a specified horizon. Report the evidence basis. It is a decision
index, not an objectively observed latent truth, a hiring probability, a
personality diagnosis, or a promise of career success.

**DEF-02 — Role versus milestone.** An occupation describes a family of work.
Its full occupational skill catalogue is distinct from a bounded initial
milestone such as completing an introductory work sample. Score the latter's
requirements while showing how it connects to the role. A high initial-fit index
does not certify readiness for the full occupation. Every score identifies the
milestone, its version, horizon, and estimation assumptions.

**DEF-03 — Skills and evidence.** A skill is an identified capability. A criterion
is an assessable requirement with explicit anchors. A project, achievement,
education record, or experience item supplies evidence for criteria; its title
alone does not prove capability. Preserve self_reported, assessment_supported,
and project_supported evidence types. Assessed and independently verified are
different properties. Unknown differs from assessed absence.

**DEF-04 — Skill gap.** A requirement not yet demonstrated in the recorded
evidence. Distinguish known gaps, unknown assessments, and missing catalogue
definitions. Recommend an assessment for an unknown, rather than asserting that
the user lacks the skill. Avoid counting one project as independent proof of
multiple criteria unless the evidence supports each criterion separately.

**DEF-05 — Market assessment.** A scoped summary of observable labour-market
signals for a role, skill, industry, or employer. Always retain geography,
period, population, source coverage, unit, classification version, and quality
limitations. Posting volume measures the collected advertisements; it does not
equal all jobs, employer profitability, or the user's prospects.

**DEF-06 — Trend, forecast, scenario.** A trend summarizes comparable historical
observations. A forecast estimates unobserved future outcomes and needs a named
target, horizon, and temporal backtest. A scenario changes disclosed assumptions
without claiming that they will occur. News and research can support a dated
AI signal but do not directly measure worker displacement or local skill demand.

**DEF-07 — Overall recommendation index.** A declared combination of personal
preparation fit F and compatible market index D. Preserve F, D, evidence coverage,
and feasibility conditions separately. A rank is relative to a stated candidate
set and objective; it is not evidence that the highest option is universally best.
User selection is a preference event, not proof of correctness or a gold label.

**DEF-08 — Industry, occupation, skill, employer.** Industry describes an
establishment's economic activity; occupation describes work; skill describes a
capability; employer identifies an organization. They are distinct dimensions.
Marginal totals cannot establish their intersections. One software role can
occur in multiple industries. Missing intersections stay unavailable.

**DEF-09 — Human-centred recommendation.** The system proposes options and
actions, explains evidence, and lets users choose. It does not make hiring
decisions. Collect concrete constraints needed for the selected scope, not
unrelated personal traits. Do not use age, gender, ethnicity, religion, names,
school prestige, or profile/reviewer IDs as personal-fit features. Evidence of
skills and actual access constraints may matter; status and stereotypes do not.

**DEF-10 — Challenge requirements.** The supplied Domain 1 brief requires intake,
an updatable AI/job-trend layer with dates and sources, explainable matching,
ordered reskilling with time estimates, meaningful role/skill links to SDGs, and
multiple disclosed future scenarios. Its data boundary is public/open/synthetic
data and synthetic demo profiles; do not use real personal data or real CVs.
Never invent jobs, courses, statistics, or source citations. At least one
relevant SDG link is required for every served role and skill; do not attach all
17 indiscriminately. Unsupported mappings remain incomplete until resolved.

**DEF-11 — Automation and evidence.** Source-derived means copied from a pinned
reference; policy-derived means deterministically transformed by a named rule;
LLM-proposed means unvalidated generated content; human-reviewed means a person
made the recorded judgment. These are distinct provenance states. An automatic
check can accept a record under policy without claiming human review.

**DEF-12 — Coverage.** Useful coverage concerns the requested population,
relevant factors, evidence reliability, time, geography, and role/industry
granularity. More rows or countries do not automatically improve a score.
Statistical relevance and causal importance require evidence; neither follows
from a factor sounding plausible. Proprietary policy defines the decision
objective, while independent observations test whether it is useful.

# Profile, role catalogue, and personal-fit policy

## Input contracts

**FIT-01 — Profile.** Collect standardized skills with assessment state and
evidence type; explicit activity/domain interests; optional projects and
achievements; education and relevant experience; weekly learning time; work
preferences and hard constraints; target country/optional region; resources,
equipment, access and learning budget; career goal. Include profile_id,
profile_group_id, profile_version, is_synthetic, collected_at, and provenance.
No fabricated default answers. Ask only for missing information material to
the selected milestone. The demo accepts synthetic cases only.

**FIT-02 — Role catalogue.** Each record contains stable role_id, title,
description, occupation identifiers, source versions, source evidence IDs,
essential/optional skill links, task/work-style tags, geography applicability,
SDG explanations, and validation status. A separate preparation milestone has
milestone_id/version, target artifact or assessment, prerequisite/task graph,
criterion IDs and nonnegative weights, evidence anchors, resource alternatives,
remaining-effort method/range, and source or policy-rule IDs for every derived
field. Full occupational requirements and milestone requirements stay separate.

Source-native role records can be automatically accepted if FIT/AUTO checks
pass. Milestone construction may also be policy-derived from compatible task
templates. Unsupported role-specific assumptions remain flagged. Never
substitute approval metadata for missing source evidence.

## Preserve the existing five features

**FIT-03 — Shared vector.** Training and serving use x=[C,I,T,R,W], finite in
[0,1]. Do not add industry, market, evidence quality, IDs, or annotation grades
as features without a versioned experiment. Projects/achievements/experience
inform criterion evidence and effort adjustments; avoid a second arbitrary
bonus for the same evidence. Education level alone is not a bonus.

**FIT-04 — Skill coverage C.** For all positive-weight milestone criteria K,
each assessed criterion has c_k in [0,1], with anchored rubric. Proposed common
anchors: 0=assessed evidence shows the milestone criterion is unmet;
0.5=specified partial evidence; 1=specified full evidence. A criterion must
define what partial/full mean; a keyword match is insufficient. Explicit
self-report may supply an assessment if the intake records the same anchored
answers, with its weaker evidence basis visible. Do not divide ordinal
beginner/intermediate/advanced labels and treat the result as a measured ratio.

Use equal criterion weight v_k=1 by default for required milestone criteria.
Optional occupational skills remain outside the scored milestone unless its
template explicitly includes them. Do not demand new handwritten weights for
every role or treat O*NET ordinal ratings as ratio weights. A different weight
must come from an accepted named template/policy override with its reason.
An empty/zero-weight milestone cannot be scored.

C_observed = sum(assessed v_k*c_k)/sum(assessed v_k).
Q = sum(assessed v_k)/sum(all v_k).
No assessed positive weight gives C_observed=null, Q=0. Score using C=C_observed
only when Q=1 within configured tolerance. Missing catalogue is invalid_catalogue;
missing user evidence is insufficient_information. Neither is user failure.

**FIT-05 — Interest alignment I.** Use explicit interest answers on relevant
activity tags, with role relevance weights from source data or a named shared
mapping policy. Preference utilities: 0=explicitly dislikes/avoids;
0.5=explicitly neutral/open; 1=explicitly interested. Weighted mean over the
complete, positive-weight tag set. These are product utility anchors, not
scientifically equal psychological distances. Unanswered is null, not neutral.
No unrecorded LLM inference from personality or writing style. Use equal tag
weights of 1 by default; a different weighting requires a named accepted policy
override, not a separate approval form for every role.

**FIT-06 — Time feasibility T.** T=min(1,h*H/E) for E>0; T=1 for known E=0;
T=null if h,H,E is unknown. h>=0 hours/week, H>0 weeks, E>=0 remaining task hours.
Use initial default H=12. Deduplicate shared prerequisite tasks. Completed tasks
can be removed only with matching progress/evidence. Do not assume that partial
criterion coverage means proportional learning time unless a task template
explicitly justifies that model. A task estimate records its source or template
assumption, uncertainty range, and method. Course duration estimates course work,
not guaranteed mastery. For an accepted effort range use E_high as the reference
and also show T_low/T_high. Unknown task effort makes aggregate E unknown.

**FIT-07 — Resource feasibility R.** Model required initial resource groups;
alternatives within a group are OR, required groups are AND. A group receives
1 if a valid accessible alternative is confirmed, 0 if every alternative is
known unavailable, and null if unresolved. R is the weighted mean of complete
group assessments. Include cost and time period for budget comparisons; a
monthly budget cannot be compared directly with lifetime course cost. A
catalogue-confirmed empty requirement set gives R=1, while an absent set is null.
Prefer verified free/low-cost alternatives before asserting infeasibility.
Required resource groups have equal weight 1 unless a named policy override
applies. Alternative items inside a group do not each receive another weight.

**FIT-08 — Work-style alignment W.** Use explicit user preferences and sourced
role/task tags. Proposed utilities: 1=compatible, 0.5=explicitly acceptable,
0=explicitly incompatible. Weighted mean of complete relevant soft-preference
assessments. A confirmed 'no preference' contributes neutral 0.5; missing answers
remain null. Use equal relevant soft-preference tag weights of 1 by default.
Hard constraints are handled before ranking, not diluted by W.

**FIT-09 — Feasibility versus suitability.** Confirmed conflicts with an explicit
user hard constraint exclude the option in that request, with a reason and
possible alternative preparation route. Unknown eligibility is 'needs_check',
not rejection. Do not infer employer/licensing requirements from an occupation
title alone. A preparation index is not a legal eligibility determination.

## Scoring and explanations

**FIT-10 — Baseline.** F0=100*(0.40C+0.20I+0.20T+0.10R+0.10W).
Preserve the foundation example x=[0.6,0.8,0.5,1,0.7] -> F0=67. These are declared
product preferences. Validate weight sum rather than silently normalizing it.
Incomplete positive-weight features return insufficient_information, known
components, Q, and the smallest useful follow-up questions. Do not silently
impute, reweight, or normalize against the current candidate list.

**FIT-11 — Learners.** Preserve Ridge/StandardScaler and CPU XGBoost candidates
for the same y in {0,1,2}, shared feature builder and served mapping
F=100*clip(y_hat/2,0,1). Keep raw y_hat for diagnostics. No architecture change
or learner replacement is justified simply by acquiring more market rows.
Artifacts must match feature, rubric, milestone/catalogue, horizon and policy
versions. Explicitly requested unavailable learners return model_not_ready.

**FIT-12 — Result and explanation.** Return F/components, milestone/horizon,
known gaps, unknown requirements, evidence IDs and evidence basis, exclusions,
scorer/version/snapshot and rule provenance. Baseline contributions are exactly
100*w_j*x_j; for learned models, baseline contributions are not explanations
of that learner. Label actual learned attribution methods and limitations.
Tie-break by stable role_id; no artificial best=100 or softmax probabilities.
Display no more numeric precision than the evidence supports; retain raw values
for reproducibility. Quantitative evidence coverage is not a confidence probability.

# Market observations, comparisons, and indexes

**MKT-01 — Separate facts.** Support role demand, skill demand, employer activity,
and industry demand as separate observation families. Optional role-by-skill or
role-by-industry slices exist only with observed joint data. Share one processing
framework across countries and industries; do not train an independent model
for every slice by default.

Every observation records source/release ID, source URL or retained raw-file
reference, source hash and row/cell, release/retrieval timestamps, observation
start/end, frequency, geography ID and level, unit/measure definition, source
population and collection/coverage regime, classification/version and entity ID,
optional observed joint dimensions, revision status, numeric value or missing
reason. Never convert a missing/suppressed observation to zero.

For observed posting data, define the family explicitly:

| Family | Numerator V | Share denominator and comparison cohort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role demand | Unique eligible advertisements assigned to the role under the declared classification | Same collected advertisement population; compare roles at a compatible level |
| Skill demand | Unique processed advertisements mentioning the skill at least once | Same fully processed advertisement population; compare skills under one extraction method |
| Employer activity | Unique eligible advertisements attributed to the resolved employer | Same collected advertisement population; compare employers with compatible observation coverage |
| Industry demand | Unique eligible advertisements assigned to the industry | Same collected advertisement population; compare industries at a compatible level |

A posting mentioning multiple skills can count once for each skill; skill shares
need not sum to one. Deduplicate postings under a recorded source-appropriate
rule, not simply identical titles. Distinguish a collected posting dataset from
an official aggregate whose provider defines the unit differently. Never merge
the two without a defensible compatible definition. Corporate-parent/branch
resolution and multi-role ads require explicit allocation rules. These are
supported schemas, not assertions that these datasets are currently acquired.

**MKT-02 — Comparison key.** A comparison requires compatible measure, unit,
frequency, geography definition, entity definition, classification, collection
method, population, denominator and dimensional grain. Historical comparisons
vary time; cross-role comparisons vary role; other dimensions remain compatible.
Comparing countries requires an explicit comparability basis, not identical code.
Country-local percentile indexes cannot establish which country has more demand.

Quarterly flows can be summed annually only with complete nonoverlapping periods
and a compatible definition; stock/rate measures need their own aggregation rule.
No annual-versus-quarter comparison, summing overlapping occupation levels,
double-counting repeated releases, or constructing joints from marginals.
Use one pinned revision per period in a snapshot. Preserve prior vintages.

**MKT-03 — Observed indicators.** For comparable counts V_t>=0:
demand share = V_role,t / V_universe,t with a compatible positive denominator;
YoY growth = (V_t/V_(t-4))-1 for comparable quarters with V_(t-4)>0.
Zero current count with positive prior gives -1; zero prior gives null/zero_base.
Unknown current or prior gives null. A count without a valid denominator can
still be displayed, but it cannot stand in for share. No implicit annualization.

**MKT-04 — Proposed observed demand/activity index.** To make the policy executable,
use a fixed, versioned comparable universe U drawn from the source, independently
of user preferences. For indicator z in that universe, define
P_U(z)=(number strictly below z + 0.5*number equal to z)/N.
Compute D=100*(0.60*P_U(demand_share)+0.40*P_U(yoy_growth)).
All records in U must have both eligible indicators. Excluded records and the
original eligible population are disclosed; freeze U and its hash per snapshot.
Use a minimum N=5 as a proposed guard against trivial relative ranks, not proof
of statistical sufficiency. All-equal values yield 0.5. No candidate-set rescaling.
The index represents relative observed demand level/growth in that cohort, not
an absolute 'future-proof' score. Display raw values, cohort size, period,
quality limitations, and the formula. Do not pool incompatible countries or
industry grains to meet the minimum. If the source cannot support this formula,
return D=null and still display valid raw observations.

This formula is a proposed product default for batch approval, not an estimate
learned from career outcomes. It activates only after source and comparability
checks. Do not silently substitute another indicator or redistribute weights.
Different measurement families require a named compatible index specification.
Ranking skill mentions requires observed skill-tagged postings; occupational
skill links can show relevance but cannot supply skill-demand counts.

Apply this template separately to role, industry, skill, or employer cohorts
only when that family's observed counts and denominators meet these definitions.
Never compare a skill index directly with an occupation index or substitute
employer activity for the role-market D in a personal recommendation. A source
publishing only selected top occupations supports only that disclosed subset;
unreported occupations are unknown. Relative ranks can remain high in a
shrinking market, so always show raw growth and avoid claiming all highly ranked
entities are expanding.

**MKT-05 — Recommendation combination.** S=0.70F+0.30D. Publish combined ranking
only when every scored role in the comparison set has a compatible D with the
same geography, period, cohort/index version, scenario and measurement scope,
and defensible occupation mappings. Otherwise rank all scored roles by F and
return ranking_mode=personal_fit_only, D=null and S=null in the ranking projection.
Preserve any available market observations in the evidence/trend view. Do not
drop market-missing roles merely to manufacture a combined leaderboard.

**MKT-06 — Mappings.** Prefer source-native IDs when they suffice. A crosswalk
relates concepts and may be exact, narrower, broader, or close. Preserve the
source's relation and cardinality. Automatic acceptance requires known source
versions, existing IDs, compatibility evidence, and allowed relation types for
the specific use. ExactMatch is not proof of current-version compatibility or
local demand. Broader/close mappings may support exploration, but cannot turn a
group-level count into an exact role count without a justified allocation model.
If the O*NET/ESCO bridge fails, use validated source-native records and leave
the bridge unavailable; do not block unrelated scoring or invent a replacement.
Taxonomy editions and database release numbers are different version axes;
different labels alone do not prove incompatibility. Check referenced IDs and
concept scope across the actual source versions rather than comparing numbers.

**MKT-07 — AI signal and future scenarios.** Store dated identifiable research,
capability/benchmark or industry-adoption signals separately from job counts,
with claim, relevant tasks, evidence type, uncertainty and extraction method.
A rapid-versus-moderate adoption scenario needs explicit named assumptions and
task/skill implications. When quantitative effect estimates are unsupported,
show qualitative scenario consequences or user-input what-if calculations;
do not label made-up growth multipliers as forecasts. Each scenario identifies
whether any quantitative rank changes are actually supported. A future learned
forecast needs target outcomes, lagged features, a naive benchmark and temporal
backtests before its outputs enter D. This is a separate component, not a new app.

**MKT-08 — Updates and attribution.** New releases create immutable snapshots,
recompute applicable comparisons/indexes and invalidate affected saved results.
They do not automatically retrain personal-fit learners. Preserve source,
attribution/reuse basis and evidence dates; acquisition does not make a dataset
reusable for every purpose. Resolve routine provenance checks automatically and
isolate unresolved records instead of freezing the entire pipeline.

# What each dataset is for, and what useful coverage means

## Dataset responsibilities

| Dataset | Supports | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O*NET occupation/task/skill references | Role definitions, task tags, criterion evidence, work contexts | Local demand or personal-fit training labels |
| ESCO occupations/skills | Standard identifiers, role-skill relationships and reusable vocabulary | Observed current skill-demand volume |
| O*NET–ESCO crosswalk | Linking compatible concepts across catalogues | User matching, current-version equivalence, Malaysian demand |
| DOSM market observations | Comparable supported Malaysian occupation/industry/time indicators | Individual ability, unobserved company/skill intersections or all vacancies |
| ILO/other country aggregates, if available | Defined comparator series and broad labour-market context | Automatically interchangeable posting counts or per-role labels |
| Skill-tagged job advertisements, when acquired | Observed skill mentions and joint role/industry/employer slices | Unbiased census of the labour market or duplicate-free vacancies by default |
| Synthetic user profiles | Intake/features/journey tests and controlled coverage of learner circumstances | A representative workforce sample or real career outcomes |
| Independently reviewed profile–role judgments | Supervised y and held-out rubric agreement | Probability of employment or long-term career success |
| Policy/LLM-generated provisional judgments | Weak supervision, workflow tests, triage or policy distillation | Independent gold labels for evaluating that same policy |
| AI research/adoption signals | Evidence-backed scenario assumptions and future-skill discussion | A direct measurement of displacement or job growth |
| Course/resource/task catalogue | Verified pathway resources, prerequisites, costs and time estimates | Guaranteed mastery or guaranteed completion time |
| Progress and assessment events | State updates, evidence changes, next-action selection and later outcome studies | Mastery merely because a task was checked off |
| Role/skill–SDG mappings | Explained links to specific SDG targets, with sources and scope | Measured social impact from choosing a role |

`config/intelligence/dataset_inventory.v1.json` distinguishes reported available,
proposed, and unsupported data. All current counts are from the user's pasted
execution report, not a fresh repository audit: 952 validated market rows
(921 numeric, 31 missing); three role drafts; five synthetic profile families;
15 blank annotation records; zero independently reviewed labels. These are not
952 personal-fit examples. The market report's 2019-Q3–2026-Q2 period is a series
range, not a claim that every occupation/industry cell is present in every quarter.

## Two learning questions, separate data

Personal-fit training asks whether features of a user–milestone pair agree with
the stated preparation-fit rubric. It needs user–role examples and independent
judgments. Market processing asks what the observed source population says
about roles/skills/industries in a geography and period. Many indicators are
computed, not trained. A future forecast would need observed future outcomes.
Do not treat all numeric source columns as one interchangeable training matrix.

## Factor relevance policy

For every candidate factor, record: decision it changes, hypothesized mechanism,
measurement definition, available evidence, missingness, geographic/temporal
scope, expected update cadence, redundancy, and evaluation plan. If it changes
no intended decision, exclude it. If it is redundant, avoid a second weight for
the same information. Adding factors to the five-feature learner requires a
versioned ablation on independent groups; plausibility is not validation.

Prioritize skills demonstrated on relevant tasks, explicit interests, transferable
evidence, milestone prerequisites, available time/resources, work preferences,
and actual target-market signals. Avoid unrelated macroeconomic columns, news
volume, prestige, or social popularity being used as personal-fit evidence.
Market context remains outside the personal-fit target.

## Coverage contract for the next gate

Build a coverage report across role/milestone, skill criterion, experience level,
career goal, hours/resources, interest/style, geography, industry, period and
evidence type. Report assessed/unknown/unsupported cells and distinct profile
families, not just row counts. Group synthetic variants and duplicates together.
For market data, also record comparable observations per series, gaps, vintage,
denominator support, and whether industry/skill intersections are observed.

Start with the actual three roles and Malaysia; preserve extensible dimensions.
The next discussion can expand the concrete coverage scope, but the engineering
gate must produce this inventory and expose gaps automatically now. The initial
five-family/15-pair packet is a calibration convenience, not a sufficient sample
size claim. No universal count can establish real-world accuracy here.

Acquire a new source only to fill a named missing decision factor or population
slice. Before promotion, show unique contribution, coverage/selection bias,
valid mapping/grain, provenance, update process, and usefulness to an actual
output. Use the smallest supported slice end to end; widen after its mechanism
works. Do not build arbitrary industry-specific coefficients from sparse data.

## Pathway and SDG completeness

Every served role needs a bounded next milestone, a valid prerequisite order,
estimated time with basis, at least one verified resource route, and sourced
role/skill links to relevant SDG targets. Incomplete fields produce explicit
partial-pathway status and an actionable missing-data reason. They need not
block grounded chat about the already valid parts. A final challenge-compliance
claim requires all six required features to be demonstrated within their stated
scope, not merely present as placeholders.

# Automatic acceptance, selective review, and model lifecycle

**AUTO-01 — Accept routine records by policy.** After package approval, automatically
accept source-native records and deterministic derivations when required fields,
hash/version, references, units, mapping applicability, criterion anchors and
semantic checks pass. Give them source-derived or policy-derived provenance,
policy_accepted status, validation evidence, rule IDs and snapshot version.
Human-reviewed status requires a real recorded human judgment. LLM-proposed
content stays provisional unless independently corroborated or a deterministic
validation rule actually establishes the field's correctness.

**AUTO-02 — Exceptions, not approval spreadsheets.** Produce one compact report
of unresolved conflicts, the affected output, evidence, attempted resolution and
recommended action. Resolve machine-checkable issues automatically. Quarantine
only affected records/features; continue other work. A person decides semantic
ambiguities only when sources and policy cannot resolve them. Deduplicate
exceptions sharing the same cause. Do not require 27 human mapping signatures
when source-native use or automated compatibility checking is sufficient.

**AUTO-03 — Transition current gates transparently.** Retain previous P2 reports
and pending files as history. Add a versioned gate definition distinguishing:
(a) catalogue/feature readiness through policy acceptance; (b) independently
reviewed calibration data; (c) empirical learner quality. A successful automated
catalogue check can clear (a); it cannot clear (b) or (c). Log the package-level
authorization and migration once. Do not rewrite old 'blocked' reports to imply
they passed earlier, and do not invent human reviewer IDs on imported records.

**EVAL-01 — Three different evaluations.** Policy conformance tests check declared
rules. Independent rubric judgments test agreement with the intended construct.
Later real-world outcome studies test whether users make useful progress.
Proprietary rules determine product behavior, not the truth of empirical claims.
Generated labels are allowed in a separately labelled distillation experiment;
test them as policy imitation, not independent recommendation accuracy.

**EVAL-02 — Small blind calibration.** Prepare the existing five synthetic profile
families across the three roles without any calculated scores/provisional grades.
The reviewer assigns 0=weak, 1=plausible, 2=strong preparation relevance with a
brief reason, or abstains when evidence is insufficient. Judge holistic fit
against the role/milestone, constraints and horizon; do not simply recompute F0
or threshold it into labels. Record group/profile/role/milestone/rubric versions,
horizon, reviewer, reason, timestamp and provenance. Only reconsider previous
judgments when a changed fact/rubric could alter them. New source rows do not
create new manual labeling duties by themselves.

Review uncertain cases and a reproducible random sample in later batches.
Uncertainty-sampled training data alone is not a representative evaluation set.
Keep a separate frozen sample covering the supported population. Report reviewer
disagreements; one reviewer's synthetic judgments are calibration, not universal
ground truth. Human calibration does not block unrelated API/chat implementation.

**EVAL-03 — Train and compare.** Preserve baseline, Ridge and XGBoost using the
same eligible features and target. Split by profile_group_id; family variants,
duplicates and related examples stay in one partition. Fit preprocessing on
training groups only. Use development-only grouped validation; a frozen holdout
is used once after selection. If too few independent groups exist, label the
result exploratory or not_evaluated. Do not fake independence with row splits.

Keep the foundation linear-gain NDCG@3 on the same candidates per profile,
MAE on served F/50 versus y, clipping/tie rates, missing-input behavior and
counterfactual checks. IDCG=0 cases are reported separately. No measured
employment probability or statistical confidence interval is implied by a fit
index. The 25-group/8-role historical suggestion is a planning target, not a gate
that magically proves sufficiency. GPU use is unnecessary for these estimators.

**EVAL-04 — Model promotion.** Until an evaluation protocol defines an acceptable
improvement and evidence supports it, keep baseline active. Hard requirements:
valid artifacts/versions, rule conformance, no group leakage, appropriate
supported scope, reproducibility and bounded latency. Report ranking quality
and errors even when they disagree with expectations. Do not choose the model
that best reproduces the policy and call that real-world validation. Reversal of
a declared monotonic relationship is a conformance defect to diagnose, not
something to conceal by adjusting the holdout. Enforce required constraints in
the actual serving path before promotion.

**EVAL-05 — Update only affected components.** New market releases -> ingest,
validate, snapshot and recompute D/ranks. New user evidence/time -> rebuild that
user's features, rescore and replan. Changed preference weights -> rescore and
version policy. Changed feature meaning -> new feature version, rebuild compatible
data and retrain affected learners. Changed rubric/target -> audit/relabel affected
examples and re-evaluate. New independent labeled data or drift -> a versioned
training run when justified. Trained estimators may need refitting; the backend,
frontend and LLM adapter do not need rebuilding. Warm-starting is optional and
must not replace validation or replay old mistakes without diagnosis.

**EVAL-06 — Execution and artifacts.** Record raw/canonical hashes, policy and
feature/role/milestone versions, group splits, estimator/preprocessor versions,
hyperparameters, actual environment, timings, evaluation status and predictions.
Keep synthetic smoke artifacts separate from promotable models. Honor the
existing project instruction to record sponsored training on RunPod when a
funded runner is available; do not claim local fits were RunPod runs, spend
unapproved funds, or block non-training implementation for runner setup.

# Groq chat and bounded personal career agent

**LLM-01 — Immediate deliverable.** A user opens a saved recommendation, asks why
a role fits or what to do next, and receives an explanation referencing the
actual result, gaps, milestone and allowed evidence. An unavailable provider
returns an explicit service state while existing numerical recommendations stay
usable. Chat implementation starts without waiting for broad dataset coverage,
new training or all independent labels.

**LLM-02 — Provider boundary.** Reuse the actual FastAPI backend and its settings.
Use a shared async LLM adapter, provider=groq, configured initial
model=openai/gpt-oss-20b, endpoint=https://api.groq.com/openai/v1.
The OpenAI Python client may be reused through its compatible base_url; reuse an
existing suitable client rather than adding two SDKs. GROQ_API_KEY is a backend
secret. No key is included in this package. Revalidate supported model features
at implementation time; account quotas are not guaranteed by a public table.

Proposed operational defaults: 20s request timeout, at most one retry of transient
provider failures within a 45s total request deadline, 1500 output-token cap,
one schema-repair attempt within the same bounded request budget, and server-side
concurrency=2. These are adjustable engineering budgets, not latency promises or
provider limits. Reuse the existing application's rate/budget controls.

**LLM-03 — Grounded context.** The backend constructs a bounded context containing
request/user scope, synthetic profile version, saved recommendation ID, scorer,
feature/policy/snapshot versions, canonical scores, component meanings, known
gaps/unknowns, selected plan/progress and an allowlist of evidence IDs. Load these
from the backend, not from untrusted client-submitted score objects. Canonical
URLs and numeric displays are rendered by the backend. Treat source documents
and user text as data; neither can redefine policy or authorize a tool.

**LLM-04 — Reply contract and validation.** Use the supplied structured-reply
projection, then validate schema, IDs, ownership, versions, evidence references
and proposed action arguments. Reject citations outside the provided evidence
set and actions outside the tool policy. A source ID existing does not prove
that every sentence is supported: keep explanations bounded to supplied claims,
use canonical fact blocks for numeric assertions, and reject unsupported factual
additions where detectable. Do not promise hallucination elimination merely
because JSON validates. The LLM cannot set score values, invent roles/courses,
change a source URL, mark mastery, alter model artifacts or supply training labels.
Expose provider/model and result freshness to the user.

Groq documentation currently lists strict structured output for the chosen model,
and documents that structured outputs do not combine with streaming/tool use in
the same request. The first chat call can therefore use a server-assembled context
and strict final response without tools. If tools are needed later, perform
bounded tool calls and a separate final structured-response step; do not assume
parallel tool calling is supported by this model. Validate content in both cases.

**LLM-05 — First agent workflow.** Career Progress Coach loads the user's selected
pathway after a progress event or due scheduled check; inspects prerequisites,
known evidence, deadlines and preferences; identifies the next permitted action;
optionally calls the same LLM for a contextual explanation; validates and saves
one proposal. Schedule determines when to run; deterministic code determines
what is allowed. There is no constant background LLM conversation.

**LLM-06 — Harness and tools.** Use small explicit Python orchestration around
existing services. Allowed reads: get_career_context (one bounded aggregate of
profile, recommendation, plan, progress and evidence), or narrow getters
get_profile, get_recommendation, get_plan, get_progress and get_evidence when
necessary. Allowed automatic writes: save_agent_suggestion and
record_agent_run. User-directed updates pass through existing validated profile,
plan and progress endpoints and recompute dependent results. Career selection,
plan replacement, mastery confirmation, external communication and training
are not autonomous tools in V1. No direct SQL or arbitrary code execution tool.

Bound runs to at most 4 tool invocations, 2 LLM requests, and 60 seconds total,
including retries/repair. Record event_id, idempotency key, input versions, policy,
tool calls, validated proposal/status and errors. Duplicate events do not create
duplicate proposals. If input versions change mid-run, discard/recompute within
the bound rather than saving a stale plan change. Ignore unchanged state during
scheduled checks, respect user-configured cadence, and persist results.

**LLM-07 — Evidence gate.** Verify grounded chat, provider failure, invalid
citations/actions, ownership and stale-result handling; a progress event must
produce one persisted suggestion using the same state shown in the UI. A live
provider call requires a configured key; otherwise record fixture checks and
live_not_exercised explicitly. A manual agent run proves its workflow, not 24/7
operation. Unattended operation additionally requires actual deployed scheduling
and an observed unattended run. Do not add a framework, vector database, MCP
server or LLM fine-tuning solely to satisfy an architecture diagram.

# Pathways, scenarios, SDG links and progress

**PATH-01 — Pathway output.** A selected role links to a defined initial milestone,
ordered tasks with prerequisites, time/cost estimates and basis, verified
resource alternatives, criterion gaps, and explainable SDG targets. Users may
choose a lower-ranked role; selection is not a positive training label. Preserve
that choice unless the user changes it. The agent supports the chosen pathway.

**PATH-02 — Task construction.** Use verified resource/task templates aligned
to unmet milestone criteria. Unknown criteria first receive a suitable assessment
task, not presumed remediation. Build a prerequisite DAG; reject cycles and
unknown prerequisites. Deduplicate shared tasks and retain each addressed
criterion link. No LLM-created course titles, arbitrary certificates or invented
prerequisite relationships. A missing template produces a specific partial-plan
reason while other validated tasks remain available.

**PATH-03 — Deterministic ordering.** Respect prerequisites and user hard
constraints first. Among currently eligible tasks, prioritize mandatory milestone
tasks, then greater addressed-criterion weight per estimated remaining hour,
then stable task_id. A criterion's weight contributes only once for each task
and must reflect the milestone's existing weights. The ratio is a scheduling
heuristic, not a proven learning optimum. Known zero-hour completed/admin tasks
are handled before dividing; unknown hours remain unscheduled with a reason.
An elective cannot replace a required task merely because its ratio is higher.

**PATH-04 — Scheduling and feasibility.** Use h hours/week and H weeks from the
profile/request. Schedule known effort using the selected estimate, conservatively
the upper bound when a range exists. Do not exceed weekly capacity. Split a
task across weeks only if its template allows it. Show remaining unscheduled
work beyond the horizon and the resulting feasibility gap. A plan exceeding
12 weeks does not imply the person cannot pursue the career. Align cost periods,
deduplicate purchased resources, and use confirmed resource alternatives. Never
pretend a feasible schedule guarantees mastery or adherence.

**PATH-05 — Provenance and changes.** Store plan_version, selected role/milestone,
profile/policy/evidence versions, tasks and chosen resources. Profile or trend
updates produce a traceable revised proposal and explanation of what changed;
do not silently replace a user's active plan. Approved direct user edits use the
existing validated services, then recompute dependent schedule/features. No
retraining is needed for an ordinary weekly-hours change.

**SCN-01 — More than one future.** Provide moderate and faster AI-adoption
scenario cards with dated source signals and explicit task-level assumptions.
Separate observed current market evidence from these future hypotheses. For
each role, show which tasks may be augmented, which preparation skills remain
relevant, what evidence supports that reasoning, and what is unknown. Do not
imply an observed or backtested disruption rate when none is available.

**SCN-02 — Quantitative what-if.** The same feature engine can compare explicit
changes such as 4 versus 8 weekly learning hours or two documented effort bounds.
Show changed inputs and recomputed T/F/plan, with other variables held fixed.
This is a deterministic sensitivity calculation, not a trained forecast of
career success. Hypothetical skill completion is marked hypothetical and cannot
be written to the actual profile as demonstrated skill.

**SCN-03 — Unsupported effects.** Without defensible quantified AI-impact
estimates, scenario consequences remain qualitative and market D unchanged.
Disclose that limitation. If credible effects are later modeled, version their
targets, assumptions and evaluation separately. No fictional multipliers to
make two scenario charts look different. Future-role matching may use sourced
emerging-role evidence without claiming a calibrated future vacancy count.

**SDG-01 — Meaningful mapping.** A role/skill link identifies one or more specific
UN SDG targets, relevant tasks, a short mechanism and evidence IDs. Distinguish
the contribution of learning/access (for example relevant skills development)
from the contribution of the eventual work product. A role title alone does not
prove climate, health or social impact.

**SDG-02 — Validation.** Check goal/target identifiers against the canonical UN
list, resolve sources and ensure the explanation fits the actual role or skill
context. Deterministic established templates can be policy-accepted; novel
LLM-generated links stay provisional until evidence supports them. Every served
role/skill needs a meaningful link for the final challenge gate. Missing mappings
are explicit incompleteness; do not attach unrelated goals to clear the gate.

**SDG-03 — Display.** Explain the link as potential contribution under stated
work circumstances. Do not compute a social-impact percentage from a selected
role or progress checkbox. Support exploration across all 17 goals while showing
only supported mappings for each particular role/skill.

**PROG-01 — State.** Track task_id, plan_version, status, timestamps, submitted
synthetic evidence, criterion assessment and its provenance. Task completion,
evidence submission and criterion mastery are different events. Only the
appropriate validated assessment changes demonstrated coverage; checking a box
can update task progress without certifying a skill.

**PROG-02 — Recalculation.** A relevant evidence/availability/resource change
rebuilds affected features and effort estimates using the same policy, then
invalidates/revises the corresponding recommendation/plan view. Irrelevant
events do not trigger unnecessary rescoring or LLM requests.

**PROG-03 — Next action.** The coach identifies the next eligible task or a
specific missing assessment/blocker, checks it against the active plan and
preferences, and saves a bounded suggestion. If nothing material changed, it
records a no-op. No motivational message substitutes for an actual next action
or creates a recurring notification without the user's configured preference.

# Contract projection semantics

The JSON schemas are additive projections to reconcile with actual Pydantic/API
contracts. They do not authorize an incompatible endpoint rewrite. Schema
validation alone does not enforce the conditions below; implementation must.

- Scored options have complete positive-weight features and Q=1 within tolerance.
  Partial evidence belongs in unscored_roles with known_components and reasons.
- personal_fit_only requires D=S=null for every scored option. A separate market
  evidence view may retain observations; do not hide the reason market ranking
  is unavailable. combined requires compatible D for every option and exact
  S=fit_weight*F+(1-fit_weight)*D within numeric tolerance.
- Sort by served F or S for the selected mode, descending, then role_id ascending.
  Do not rescale scores according to candidate membership. options contain only
  scored, constraint-eligible roles. Unscored/excluded records retain reason codes.
- unavailable has no scored options. model_not_ready is explicit for a requested
  learner without a compatible artifact. It does not silently execute baseline.
- Profile/role/milestone/policy/evidence versions refer to immutable known records.
  Evidence IDs on options must belong to the result's authorized evidence set.
- All fixture IDs in examples.synthetic.json are invented for contract checks;
  never import them into production catalogues or advertise them as real sources.
- LLM proposed action role/target IDs must exist in the user's authorized scope.
  recommendation_id must refer to the context the backend supplied. User text
  cannot substitute another user's record. Citation inclusion is not proof that
  a sentence is factually supported; enforce bounded claim/evidence handling.
- Structured replies never contain authoritative score fields. The UI obtains
  scores and URLs from the saved backend result, not by parsing answer text.
- Keep full source/time/geography/cohort/mapping metadata in referenced evidence
  records. A compact projection does not remove provenance obligations.

Useful reason codes include missing_assessment, missing_interest,
missing_effort_estimate, missing_resource_status, missing_work_preference,
invalid_catalogue, incompatible_mapping, unsupported_market_grain,
incompatible_market_scope, missing_yoy_prior, zero_yoy_base,
missing_pathway_resource, missing_sdg_mapping, hard_constraint_conflict,
stale_input_version, model_not_ready and provider_unavailable. Map to existing
typed error conventions and return user-readable explanations.

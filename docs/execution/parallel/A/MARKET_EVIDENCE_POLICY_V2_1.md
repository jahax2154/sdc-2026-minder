## Bounded ranking interpretation

A supported related-group signal adds **at most +1** to the existing final
within-group current comparison, or to F for otherwise personal-only exploration.
This is a small, explicitly provisional prior for exploration of a related
critical occupation family, not ad volume, verified opportunity or competence.
A separately supported exact designation could receive at most +2; **none** is
currently supplied. Multiple paths use the maximum once; no accumulated bonuses.
Missing/unmapped evidence has no adjustment and is never coded as no shortage.

Current comparison remains `.85*F + .15*M`, followed by that bounded contextual
adjustment and clipping to 0..100. F and the employer/published normalizations
from v2 are unchanged. Sector totals contribute zero; forecasts contribute zero.
No source designation enters an advertisement-count denominator or training target.
Publication date and continuous observation window of MyCOL remain null; actual
availability is preserved separately from the 2024/2025 edition label. No fabricated
calendar dates, current hiring, qualification or replacement probability appears.

Source selection now maximises coverage across valid independent activity groups,
then prefers published activity among equally covered assignments. This fixes a
v2 engineering edge case: an available employer comparison could be stranded by
a singleton published category. For example, Software Developers/Accountants/Data
Scientists can use their three-member employer cohort rather than leaving one out
behind a two-member published cohort. Unlike source types are never pooled.
